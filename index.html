Privacy Policy for LexoNews

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

LexoNews is built as a free, open-source custom feed reader application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use completely as-is.

1. Absolute On-Device Data Isolation

Your privacy is an absolute architectural design choice of LexoNews. The application operates entirely locally on your physical device. All custom search operators, complex filter criteria, content processing rules, text-scoring configurations, and downloaded content caches are stored inside an isolated SQLite database on your device via the Room persistence layer. None of this data is ever transmitted, collected, uploaded, or exposed to any external servers, third parties, or systems.

2. Network and External Connectivity

LexoNews establishes direct HTTPS network connections directly to the public RSS or XML feed targets specified by you. No intermediary proxy server is utilized. The application does not deploy any tracking cookies, analytical event loops, behavioral frameworks, or external logging monitors.

3. Data Deletion and Control

Because your configuration profile lives exclusively on your device, you possess total sovereignty over it. Clearing the application's cache or data via the Android System Settings panel—or completely uninstalling the application—instantly and permanently purges every trace of data from the device's storage media.

4. Changes to This Policy

This privacy documentation may be modified periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any structural changes.
